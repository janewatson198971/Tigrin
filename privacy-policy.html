1. Information we collect

We do not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, videos, audio files, documents, or precise GPS location to use the app.

  • App activity and usage data: app launches, sessions, screen views, app interactions, and other in-app events.
  • Device or other identifiers: Advertising ID, app instance identifiers, installation identifiers, AppsFlyer ID, Firebase-related identifiers, and similar identifiers.
  • Approximate location: general location such as country, region, or city derived from the device IP address.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, network information, and crash or diagnostic information where applicable.
  • Purchase-related events: if the app offers in-app purchases or subscriptions, purchase events may be processed for analytics, attribution, and app performance measurement.

2. How we use information

The information described above is used for the following purposes:

  • Analytics: to understand how users interact with the app and to improve app performance and user experience.
  • Advertising or marketing: to measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns, attribute installs, and analyze campaign performance.
  • Fraud prevention, security, and compliance: to detect suspicious activity, prevent attribution fraud, protect the app, and comply with applicable requirements.
  • App functionality: to support basic app operation, diagnostics, and performance monitoring where applicable.
